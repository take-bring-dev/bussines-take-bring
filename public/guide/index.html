    How Take & Bring Business works

    A complete, plain-language guide to the EU freight marketplace: who it is for, how a load moves from publish to payment, and what Super Admin sees behind the scenes.

    ● Live product logic Shipper · Carrier · Admin Escrow · CMR · Notifications

    1. What is this?

    Take & Bring Business is a digital freight marketplace for industrial shippers and verified carriers across the EU. It is not a classic freight broker that marks up every load. Instead:

    • A shipper publishes a load (pickup → delivery, cargo, vehicle class).
    • Carriers bid with transparent net prices.
    • The shipper awards one offer.
    • Money is held in escrow until delivery is confirmed.
    • A Super Admin watches the whole platform (KYC, loads, escrow, timeline).
    In one sentence Shipper posts → carriers compete → shipper picks a winner → escrow locks funds → transit → deliver → payout.

    4. Step-by-step tutorial

    Walk through one load as if you were training a new teammate:

    Register & KYC

    Users sign up as shipper or carrier. Documents go through KYC. Super Admin approves or rejects in /admin/kyc. Until approved, marketplace access stays limited.

    Admin decides Shipper Carrier

    Shipper publishes a load

    From /s/loads/new: route, windows, weight, vehicle class, cargo notes. Status becomes PUBLISHED / RECEIVING_OFFERS. All active carriers get a “new load” alert. Admins see “Shipper published a load”.

    Shipper acts Notify carriers + admins

    Carriers place bids

    On the open freight board /c, a carrier submits a price. The shipper is notified (“New offer on your load”). Admin timeline: “Carrier submitted an offer”.

    Carrier acts

    Shipper awards one offer

    On the load detail page, shipper accepts one bid. Load → ASSIGNED. Winner gets “Offer accepted”. Losers get “Offer not selected”. An EscrowPayment is created as AWAITING_PAYMENT.

    Shipper acts Create escrow

    Fund escrow (billing)

    Shipper pays via /s/billing (Stripe Checkout or mock mode). Escrow moves to AUTHORIZED (funds held, not yet paid out). Carrier is told escrow is funded.

    Shipper pays

    In transit

    Carrier marks pickup / in transit on /c/loads/[id]. Load → IN_TRANSIT. Shipper and admin are notified. Carrier can also request payment attention if escrow is still awaiting.

    Carrier acts

    Deliver & capture

    Carrier marks DELIVERED. System captures escrow → CAPTURED. Carrier sees funds on /c/earnings. Admin timeline records delivery. Contract closed.

    Carrier confirms Capture escrow

    5. Load & escrow statuses

    Load lifecycle

    StatusMeaning
    DRAFTSaved but not on the market
    PUBLISHED / RECEIVING_OFFERSVisible to carriers; bids allowed
    ASSIGNEDOne carrier won; preparing pickup
    IN_TRANSITGoods on the road
    DELIVEREDCompleted; escrow capture runs
    CANCELLED / EXPIREDStopped or timed out

    Escrow lifecycle

    StatusMeaning
    AWAITING_PAYMENTAward done; shipper must fund
    AUTHORIZEDMoney held (manual capture later)
    CAPTUREDReleased after delivery

    6. Notifications & badges

    The product uses one notification path (database + optional FCM push):

    • Bell drawer in shipper / carrier / admin chrome (no separate “notifications page”).
    • Clicking a notification opens the exact detail URL (load, billing, earnings, admin load, etc.).
    • Unread count updates the bell badge and the PWA / home-screen icon badge.
    • Push deep links use NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL (must be your real domain on Vercel, not localhost).
    Admin timeline Super Admins get fan-out events for publish, bid, award, transit, and deliver — visible on /admin and /admin/activity.

    8. Practice walkthrough (15 minutes)

    1. Open three browser profiles (or windows): Admin, Shipper, Carrier.
    2. Log in with the three seed accounts above.
    3. As shipper: publish a load Hamburg → Rotterdam.
    4. As admin: confirm the timeline shows “Shipper published a load”.
    5. As carrier: open the board, place a bid.
    6. As shipper: accept the bid → fund escrow on Billing.
    7. As carrier: mark In transit, then Delivered.
    8. As admin: open Escrow monitor and the load detail — status should be captured / delivered.
    9. Click a notification in each role — you should land on the related page, not a blank feed.
    Success criteria You can explain the six-step flow without looking at this page, and you know which sidebar each role uses.
